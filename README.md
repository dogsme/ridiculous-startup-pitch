## The pitch

We buy abandoned half-finished knitting, model kits and puzzles for pennies, then resell them as finish-it kits to crafters paying $29 a month for the joy of finishing without starting.

It sounds like a joke — who buys someone else's unfinished hobby? — until you look at the market: millions of half-finished projects sit in cupboards, their owners glad to be rid of the guilt for pennies, while other crafters love the satisfying last stretch of a project and hate the blank first page. Cheap supply, a recurring subscription, and a customer who pays for the feeling of finishing.
